REFERENCE
N
NDVI · vegetation greenness

Uses near-infrared and red reflectance (B08, B04). Useful for crop and vegetation condition screening.

W
NDWI · open water screening

Green and near-infrared (B03, B08) highlight many open-water features; shadows and turbid water can complicate interpretation.

M
NDMI · vegetation moisture proxy

Near-infrared and short-wave infrared (B08, B11). B11 is 20 m, so resampling and alignment matter.

Interpretation, not ground truthClouds, haze, shadows, seasonal effects, mixed pixels and atmospheric correction can change results. Validate any operational conclusion with field data.

Repeatable comparison protocol
A defensible workflow for Bangladesh study areas
01
Match season and study boundary

Compare similar seasonal windows and consistent boundaries to reduce phenology and geometry bias.

02
Mask clouds, cirrus and shadows

Use the Sentinel-2 Scene Classification Layer (SCL) or a validated cloud mask; record the valid-pixel share.

03
Harmonize resolution

NDVI bands are 10 m; indices using B11 are 20 m. Document resampling strategy and projection.

04
Compute AOI statistics

Use median / percentiles, valid-pixel count and uncertainty notes; do not infer change from scene cloud percentages alone.

Demo series disclaimer

The trend chart and change delta are synthetic examples to demonstrate the interface. They are not official observations or an estimate of environmental change at any specific Bangladesh location.
